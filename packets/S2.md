## Goal (the player-visible gate)
The player can **select** the harvester (single click AND drag a selection box), see a **selection ring** on it,
**right-click a destination** to issue a move order, and see an **order-confirmation marker** flash at the target.
Camera can **pan** (arrow keys or edge/drag). Nothing more — no combat, no base building, no production.

## Contract / architecture to use (do not reinvent)
- **All screen→world/tile conversion goes through `coords.ts`**: `screenToWorld`, `screenToTile`, `worldToScreen`.
  `src/view/input.ts` reads mouse pixels and converts via these — no inline pixel↔tile math (it's a red-flag per
  the constitution).
- **Input is intent, not mutation.** `src/view` (wall-clock/DOM allowed) captures clicks and pushes plain
  **command intents** (e.g. `{type:'select', rect}` / `{type:'move', target}`) into a queue on the view side;
  the **`command` SimSystem** (runs first in `SYSTEM_ORDER`) drains that queue each tick and applies them to sim
  state (set `selected`, set `movement.target`). The sim itself never reads the DOM. Keep the queue handoff simple
  and deterministic (drain in FIFO order).
- Selection is an **additive component field** (e.g. `selected: boolean`, or a `selectable` component). Box-select
  selects player units whose world position falls inside the dragged rect (convert rect corners via `screenToWorld`).
- A move order sets the selected unit's `movement.target` (reuse the S0 movement system). For the harvester, a
  manual move order may interrupt its FSM for S2 — keep it simple; full order/FSM arbitration is later.
- **Order-confirmation marker:** on issuing a move, spawn a short-lived visual marker at the target (a ring that
  fades over ~0.5s), drawn by the renderer. This is the "orders confirm on screen" half of the gate (§5.8).
